FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What international clients usually ask before we start working together.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan? +

Yes — I currently work with clients in the UK (Citywise Property Solutions) alongside Pakistan-based brands, and I'm actively taking on new clients across the US, UK, Canada and Europe. All communication, reporting and strategy work is done in English, remotely.

What platforms and tools do you work in? +

Shopify, WordPress, Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager/Business Suite, GA4, Google Search Console, Google Tag Manager, Google Merchant Center and Google Business Profile.

Can you handle SEO and paid ads together? +

Yes — this is where most of my client work sits. Running SEO and paid media separately wastes budget; I plan them as one funnel so paid traffic and organic growth reinforce each other.

How do you start working with a new international client? +

We start with a free strategy call to review your current site, ads (if any) and market. From there I propose a scope with clear deliverables and KPIs before any retainer begins.

What industries have you worked in? +

Healthcare/aesthetics, property & compliance, e-commerce (skincare, fragrance, home & lifestyle, appliances), automotive and kitchen appliances.
