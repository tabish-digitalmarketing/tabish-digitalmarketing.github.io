My Digital Marketing Journey

From a Computer Science degree and early media work to SEO, Google Ads and Meta Ads — how 15+ years of technology and digital growth work came together.

Syed Muhammad Tabish · Performance Marketing Consultant

My path into digital marketing didn't start with marketing at all — it started with a Computer Science degree and an early career touching technology and media, including time around broadcast and YouTube audience growth with HUM TV. That mix gave me an odd but useful lens: I understood systems and audiences before I understood ad platforms.

Finding My Way Into Performance Marketing

The shift into performance marketing happened gradually, project by project. Early SEO work taught me how search demand actually behaves. Managing Google Ads and Meta campaigns taught me how paid acquisition either complements or fights that organic demand. Working inside Shopify stores taught me that traffic without conversion optimization is just an expensive vanity metric.

Over 15+ years, that combination has shaped how I approach every project now: diagnose first, then decide which channels — search, paid, local, or on-site — actually deserve the budget.

A Few Projects Along the Way

Working with SL Aesthetics Clinic, a multi-location aesthetic clinic in Pakistan, sharpened my approach to local SEO and multi-location Google Business Profile strategy — a very different discipline from single-location SEO.

MaxDeals.pk pushed me deeper into commercial keyword research for Pakistan's appliances market, balancing search volume against real buyer intent rather than vanity traffic.
